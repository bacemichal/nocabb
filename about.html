About Nocabb

Welcome on this website. My name is Matej Kapusta, I am youngsta from Slovakia. My whole life I had a creative personality and I always loved fashion. One day I realized it really became my passion so I decided to start my own brand No.Cabb. It was 2,5 years of designing, cutting, sewing but mainly making mistakes until I found my own personal style that my soul fell in love with. It was a long journey until my clothes became fully wearable.

Nowadays I’m building garments that are really reflecting my feelings. I love to use fabrics with unusual prints, something you cannot find normally on the streets. Each piece is fully handmade from scratch and One n Only of their kind aka. (1 of 1).

Currently: Netherlands, Oss
